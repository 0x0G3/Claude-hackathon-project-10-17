Next Door · client framework · draft Oct 17

The promise

A person leaving care walks out with one next step, in their language.

The person talks to the screen. The tool checks for danger first, then finds one door from the checked list. A care manager reviews it. The person takes it home on paper, as a text draft, or on their phone. Nothing is ever sent.

Who holds the device

The person leaving care, on their own phone or a shared IHH tablet, often with a care manager beside them. Some have no phone. Some read little English. Some are scared.

So: big type, one idea per screen, no login, works offline once loaded, and every result can be printed.

Words · rules every screen keeps

Short. Under 15 words a sentence. About grade 5. Likely, never sure. “Likely a fit. Not a promise.” Never “you qualify.” Sourced. Every phone, hour and rule shows its check date and source. Unknown says so. No hours listed? “Ask about hours when you call.” Their words first. Spanish on top, English below for staff, on the printout only. No pity. Say “people”, not “clients” or “the homeless”. No exclamation marks.
