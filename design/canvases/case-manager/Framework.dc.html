Who and when

A case manager with eight people, five minutes before rounds.

Desktop at the station, tablet on the floor. Opens Today, sees who needs them first, acts, and gets back to people.

Four jobs, one screen each

SpotToday. Needs you now, numbers, the board.
ReviewPick the door, see why it ranked, approve.
Keep safeSafety steps. Search stays off.
Follow upDid the door work? Feeds the gaps view. Next.

Who comes first

  1. Safety flags, always on top, navy.
  2. Leaving in 5 days or less without a plan, soonest first.
  3. No sure door, because they need your time, not a listing.
  4. Plans ready to review.
  5. Everyone else, on the board.

Review rules

  • Every match says why it ranked where it did.
  • Unknown fields read “Not stated by source”, never blank.
  • Staff can swap the door. The preview and text update with it.
  • Nothing reaches the person until Approve. Nothing ever sends.
  • Anything the listings can’t answer becomes a “Check” to ask on the call.
