Every person leaves knowing which door to walk through today.

Orange County has programs for most needs. People get stuck finding the one that fits, knowing if they qualify, and making the first call. Next Door turns a discharge into one plain-language plan. Every fact comes from a checked listing. Every need with no good door is logged.

Roadmap for IHH

Start small at one site, prove care managers use it without being asked, then let the gaps data decide what IHH builds or funds next. Each phase ends with a go or no-go decision.

Today

Working prototype

  • 5 screens on the 40 checked listings, in the IHH design system
  • Guardrails: no invented facts, safety stop, medical redirect
  • Safety exit, gap state and Spanish plan card
  • Gaps view from 12 sample clients
DecisionIs this worth a 4-week pilot?
Weeks 1–4

Pilot at one center

  • 2–3 care managers, made-up or consented cases only
  • IHH owns and reviews the resource list; add a verified DV hotline
  • Claude matching that must cite a listing for every fact
  • Spanish and Vietnamese plans
DecisionDo care managers open it before discharge without a reminder?
Months 2–3

Close the loop

  • Record follow-up: did the client reach the door?
  • Monthly gaps report to leadership
  • Wider directory through the national 211 API, with permission
  • Re-verify listings on a set schedule
DecisionWhich gap do we fund or partner on first?
Months 4–6

Scale and act on gaps

  • Roll out to all recuperative care sites
  • Privacy and IT review before any link to IHH case records
  • Use gaps evidence in grants and partner asks, such as small loans and travel home
DecisionBuild in-house, or hand to a partner?
What we need from IHH to start
  • One pilot champion at a recuperative care center
  • One owner for the resource list
  • Two hours of care manager feedback in week 1
  • How IHH handles small loans and travel home today
